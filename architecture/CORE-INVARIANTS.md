# Aurora-Lens Core Invariants

These are public architectural invariants, not implementation instructions.

1. **A candidate is not a commitment.** Generation, retrieval, availability, repetition, or prior assertion does not itself confer standing.
2. **Admissibility precedes consequence.** A candidate may become operative only when the applicable conditions permit the transition.
3. **Authority is independent of the proposer.** A model or application cannot make its own output authoritative merely by producing or endorsing it.
4. **Unresolved state may persist.** Ambiguity, contradiction, stale authority, incomplete identity, and insufficient evidence need not be forced into a determination.
5. **Identity and relationships require standing.** Similarity or contextual likelihood does not authorise transferring attributes, permissions, or history between entities.
6. **Freshness concerns present validity.** Previously valid evidence or authority may require revalidation before renewed reliance.
7. **Non-commitment is a governed result.** Clarification, containment, revision, refusal, and stop are legitimate outcomes.
8. **Continuation does not reopen a closed commitment.** A system may continue lawfully without converting a prohibited determination into permission.
9. **Deployment scope does not redefine capability.** A host may use one governance surface without shrinking the architecture's wider commitment-admissibility envelope.
10. **Audit evidence belongs to the decision path.** The basis for a governance outcome should be reconstructable from governed state and recorded conditions, not invented after the event.
