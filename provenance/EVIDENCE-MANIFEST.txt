Scope
-----
This manifest records SHA-256 fingerprints for six retained copies identified by the applicant as filed Australian provisional specifications, one private offline verification bundle, and one private development note. The underlying files are not included in the public repository.

A hash permits a later-produced file to be tested for byte identity with the fingerprint recorded here. A hash alone does not establish the file's contents, filing date, legal status, authorship, or correspondence with a patent-office copy. Those propositions require the relevant document and filing record.

The public date of this manifest is established by the eventual repository commit or other public deposit containing it, not merely by the preparation date written above.
