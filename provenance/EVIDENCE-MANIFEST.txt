Scope
-----
This manifest records SHA-256 fingerprints for six retained copies identified
by the applicant as filed Australian provisional specifications, one private
offline verification bundle, and one private development note.

The underlying private files are not included in the public repository.

The provisional applications represented here form part of the filing history
preceding later Australian and United States non-provisional/complete
applications for the Aurora-Lens architecture and related runtime governance
mechanisms.

A hash permits a later-produced file to be tested for byte identity with the
fingerprint recorded here. A hash alone does not establish the file's contents,
filing date, legal status, authorship, inventorship, priority entitlement, or
correspondence with a patent-office copy. Those propositions require the
relevant document and filing record.
