# Risk Matrix with Qualifying Notes

| Risk tier | Base response time | Approval requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Low | 72 hours | Team lead sign-off |
| Medium | 24 hours | Manager sign-off |
| High | 4 hours | Director sign-off |

## Qualifying prose

The base response-time table applies only during normal business operations.

During declared peak events, Medium tier may be treated as High tier for triage routing.

If statutory deadlines apply, statutory time limits override table values.
