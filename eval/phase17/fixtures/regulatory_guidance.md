# Operational Guidance Note RG-17

## 1. Defined terms

- **Critical Incident**: an event with service impact grade 3 or higher.
- **Responsible Officer**: the shift lead assigned to incident command.

## 2. Obligations

1. The Responsible Officer must open an incident record within 15 minutes.
2. A customer-facing notice must be issued for Critical Incidents.
3. A post-incident review must be completed within 5 business days.

## 3. Exceptions

- If legal hold is active, external publication may be delayed.
- If law enforcement requests confidentiality, notices may be restricted to authorised channels.
