# Public Service Operations Standard

## Purpose

Define intake, triage, and escalation responsibilities for frontline services.

## Procedure

1. Intake agent records request details and category.
2. Triage officer assigns priority within 30 minutes.
3. Case owner resolves standard requests within SLA.

## Responsibilities

- Intake agent: completeness check.
- Triage officer: priority and route.
- Duty manager: approve emergency escalations.

## Escalation conditions

Escalate to duty manager when:

- safety risk is identified,
- statutory deadline is within 24 hours,
- unresolved blocker persists beyond one business day.
